Frequently asked questions

How do I place an order?

Choose a product, add it to your cart, then enter your delivery details and preferred date at checkout. You do not need to create an account.

Can I add a personal message?

Yes. There is a message box at checkout, and we will include it on a card with your gift.

Can I choose the delivery date and time?

Yes. Pick a date and a time slot at checkout. Same-day delivery depends on the cut-off time shown on our Delivery page.

Do you deliver cakes and flowers together?

Yes. Our combos are delivered together in one order, and you can also add a bouquet and a cake to the same cart.

How can I pay?

Payment options will be shown at checkout, such as cash on delivery, card or bank transfer, depending on what the store has set up.

What if the recipient is not home?

Please give us an alternative contact number in the message box. We will try to reach the recipient before leaving the order with a neighbour or returning it.
